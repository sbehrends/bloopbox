The bloopbox Brand Kit: Final Foundations
1. The Core Visual Language
Here is the structured prompt for our foundational visual asset based on your selection.

Subject & Purpose
The primary brand identity mark and logotype for bloopbox, a screenless, tactile WhatsApp messenger for toddlers, defining the Retro-Futurist Intercom visual language.

Visual Description
A high-resolution, precise brand sheet displaying the final logo on a soft cream matte background. The primary logo mark is the Retro-Futurist Intercom icon: a stylized, slightly simplified arcade cabinet silhouette (seen in profile), where the central screen is replaced by three distinct, large, circular buttons that slightly overlap, colored teal, sunny yellow, and coral pink, and accented by sound wave lines. Below the icon, the wordmark "bloopbox" is rendered in the distinctive Retro-Futurist chunky, rounded, geometric font, with generous kerning and thick letterforms. The lighting is soft and directional, as if on a high-end specifications document.

Brand Consistency Markers
Palette: Electric Teal (#00A7E1), Sunny Yellow (#F7B733), Coral Pink (#FF6F61), Deep Navy Text (#1A2B48), Soft Cream Background (#F7F4EF).
Aesthetic Mood: Bold, playful, tactile, screenless, friendly, retro-modern, geometric.
Typography: A custom, ultra-bold, rounded geometric sans-serif that looks like it belongs on a retro-futuristic arcade panel.

Context & Rationale
This logo perfectly bridges the device's technical capabilities (sound waves, intercom form) with its physical user experience (large, tactile, color-coded buttons). The chunky font and primary palette reassure parents that the tech is playful and approachable for little humans, not complex and "digital."

2. Final Primary Palette
Main Accent: Electric Teal (#00A7E1) — Energetic, modern connection.

Secondary Accent: Sunny Yellow (#F7B733) — Joy, warmth, classic arcade cue.

Tertiary Accent: Coral Pink (#FF6F61) — Fun, approachability, and soft pop.

Primary Text: Deep Navy (#1A2B48) — Trusted, stable communication.

Base: Soft Cream (#F7F4EF) — Soft on the eyes, avoids stark white, premium feel.
